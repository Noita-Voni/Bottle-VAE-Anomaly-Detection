# AI Declaration

OpenAI AI tools assisted with the following areas of this project:

- Conceptual explanations and discussions of preprocessing, image resolution, latent dimensions, VAE architecture, loss functions, and experimental design.
- Developing, refining, and troubleshooting preprocessing, model, training, anomaly-scoring, heatmap, and evaluation code.
- Organising documentation, explaining recorded results, and drafting or editing research-paper text.

This assistance supported learning and improved efficiency. Reported experimental results came from the project's saved training and evaluation runs using the actual MVTec AD bottle dataset. No synthetic dataset images or fabricated experimental measurements were introduced.
